# Rewrite Library Lines

## What Success Looks Like

Rewritten resume lines that seamlessly adopt the phrasing, vocabulary, and active language of the target job description, while strictly preserving the integrity, facts, and metrics of the original accomplishments.

## Your Approach

1. **Vocabulary Mapping**:
   - Extract core verbs, technical terms, and phrases from the target job description (e.g., if the JD highlights *"cross-functional orchestration"*, note that verb/noun combination).
2. **Translate Bullets**:
   - Take selected lines from the master resume YAML.
   - Refreeze the bullet: keep the exact outcome/metric and context, but swap in the vocabulary of the JD.
   - **Example**:
     * *Original*: "Led a team of 5 developers to deliver a client portal ahead of schedule."
     * *Rewritten*: "Orchestrated a cross-functional team of 5 developers to accelerate delivery of the client portal."
3. **Verify Outcome Focus**:
   - Double-check that every rewritten line still follows the active formula: **[Active Verb] + [Context/Project] + [Result/Metric]**.
   - Strip any passive voice or helper verbs (e.g., swap "was responsible for managing" to "managed").
4. **Present Comparison**:
   - Show the owner the original line and the rewritten line so they can verify that no facts were changed.

## Memory Integration

* Check `BOND.md` for any specific writing style rules (e.g., preferred verbs, punctuation rules, or words to avoid).

## Wrapping Up

* Present the proposed rewrites to the owner.
* Confirm: *"Do these rewritten lines accurately reflect your actual work while capturing the JD's phrasing?"*
* When complete, present the final options to the owner and proceed to harvest approved edits.
